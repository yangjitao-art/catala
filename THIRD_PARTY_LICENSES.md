## What must accompany a binary release

1. `LICENSE.txt` (Apache-2.0) and `NOTICE` (modification statement).
2. The license text of every LGPL component and its linking exception. Run this script with `--bundle DIR` to collect them.
3. The LGPL text and replaceability note for `libgmp` if it is bundled rather than taken from the operating system.
4. The Unicode License v3 notice for the generated transliteration table.

Machine-readable bill of materials: `sbom.cdx.json`.
